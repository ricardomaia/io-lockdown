I/O Lockdown

Fail-Close Endpoint Protection for Windows Workstations

Strategic Physical Defense

I/O Lockdown mitigates attack vectors like BadUSB, Hardware Keyloggers, and Rogue Network Adapters by enforcing a strict security posture when your system is unattended.

Hardware Kill-Switch

Detects unauthorized physical changes and instantly disables USB controllers and network adapters.

Bluetooth Smart Lock

Automatically locks your workstation when your paired device (phone/watch) goes out of range.

Intruder Evidence

Captures visual evidence via webcam when a hardware tampering attempt is detected.
