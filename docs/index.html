Fail-Close Architecture

Zero-trust applied to physical ports. When the workstation locks, the digital drawbridge is raised, severing all potential exfiltration vectors.

USB Neutralization

Instant USBSTOR revocation upon lock state entry.

Network Kill-Switch

Software-defined network isolation during unauthorized presence states.

Policy_Engine.cs
// State Enforcement
public async Task OnWorkstationLock() {
  await NIC.SetState(Status.Disabled);
  await Registry.BlockKey("USBSTOR");
}

// Tamper Detection
void OnDeviceInserted(Device d) {
  if (IsLocked) EmergencyShutdown();
}

Operational Use Cases

I/O Lockdown is most valuable in environments where Windows endpoints remain physically accessible, temporarily unattended, or exposed to shared operational spaces. It complements EDR, antivirus, NAC, and group policies by enforcing physical-port security during locked or unattended states.

Healthcare & Imaging Rooms

Protects radiology, PACS, modality consoles, and clinical workstations where sensitive patient data may be exposed during shift changes, unattended periods, or technician movement.

Mitigates: USB exfiltration, rogue network adapters, unauthorized DICOM copy.
Value: privacy hardening for LGPD-sensitive clinical environments.

Shared Corporate Workstations

Adds fail-close protection for call centers, NOCs, SOCs, help desks, and multi-user terminals where machines may remain logged in but briefly unattended.

Mitigates: BadUSB payloads, opportunistic tampering, unauthorized peripherals.
Value: reduces the attack window between user absence and session recovery.

R&D, Engineering & IP Labs

Helps protect source code, PCB projects, firmware, CAD files, research datasets, and prototypes in labs where visitors, contractors, or insiders may have physical proximity.

Mitigates: data theft, hardware keyloggers, stealth device insertion.
Value: preserves intellectual property and captures evidence of tampering.

Government, Defense & Forensics

Strengthens restricted workstations used in public-sector, defense, incident response, and digital forensics environments where physical compromise is a realistic threat model.

Mitigates: PoisonTap-style attacks, rogue NICs, session hijacking attempts.
Value: creates a physical tamper boundary with local evidence capture.

Financial & Trading Desks

Protects high-value terminals used for banking operations, treasury desks, trading floors, and payment environments where active sessions and network trust are high-impact assets.

Mitigates: credential interception, rogue gateway injection, quick physical implants.
Value: limits damage from short unattended windows.

Mobile Executives & Field Teams

Adds protection for notebooks used in airports, hotels, coworking spaces, client sites, and other semi-public environments where physical access can occur quickly.

Mitigates: unattended laptop tampering, USB network attacks, drive-based exfiltration.
Value: Bluetooth proximity locking reduces dependence on user discipline.

Industrial, OT & Critical Infrastructure Terminals

Provides a defensive layer for Windows-based SCADA, production, automation, and operator workstations where removable media and technician access are common operational risks.

Threat USB malware, rogue devices, and unauthorized maintenance peripherals.
Control Session-aware I/O isolation, PnP tamper detection, and persistent violation state.
Outcome Reduced exposure for hard-to-patch systems and physically reachable endpoints.

Operational Hazard

Accidental triggers or hardware failures during Lockdown state will sever all USB communication. Recovery requires a Physical Reboot.
